About Elev8 Academy Empowering Young Minds. Building Bright Futures.

At Elev8 Academy, we believe every child has the potential to achieve great things. Our expert tutoring programmes in Maths and English are designed to build confidence, spark curiosity, and help students reach their full potential.

Our Mission

To provide high-quality, personalised tutoring that inspires students to learn, grow, and succeed — in school and beyond.

Our Values

Student-Centered

We focus on each child's unique needs, strengths, and learning goals.

Excellence

We are committed to delivering the highest quality tutoring.

Integrity

We build trust through honesty, transparency, and respect.

Growth Mindset

We encourage curiosity, perseverance, and a love for learning.

At Elev8 Academy,
we’re here to empower students across Aotearoa New Zealand to succeed, thrive, and truly enjoy their academic journey.

We work with primary, intermediate, students, helping them navigate challenges, build confidence and achieve the grades they aspire to.

As our logo proudly says — elevate! — we aim to lift not just academic performance but also a student’s belief in their own potential.

Every lesson is designed to be engaging and fruitful, equipping students with the skills and mindset to succeed in school and beyond.

Here at Elev8 Academy we aim to help our kiwi kids to thrive in academia through the guidance of experienced tutors.

Our team aims to build your child’s confidence and allow your child to have a fruitful academic journey!

Academia isn’t just about studying for the final exam, rather the aim is to prepare your children to tackle real world problems and gain better understanding and knowledge of practical work.

Because at Elev8 Academy, we believe academics aren’t just about grades — they’re the foundation for opportunities and success in the real world.
